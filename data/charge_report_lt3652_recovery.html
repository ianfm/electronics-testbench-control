Phases

Setup

  • Solar input: B2902B CH1 replaying the measured panel curve panel-iv-2026-08-24_145423 (Voc 7.71 V, Isc 0.194 A, Pmax 1.098 W at 6.25 V). Level parked at Voc, compliance re-derived from the achieved voltage at ~10 Hz.
  • Battery current: CH2 in series with battery+, 0 V source, 3 A range, no clamp below the instrument maximum. Both channel LOW terminals floated (:OUTP:LOW FLO).
  • Battery voltage: Keysight 34461A directly across the pack, >10 GΩ input.
  • Pack: protected LiFePO4, 1350 mAh, protection latched (terminals 0 V) at start. Board on power-floor firmware.
  • Sequence: solar on first, battery loop closed second. End of charge = current below 20 mA for 5 min.

Reading the numbers

  • Efficiency is the ratio of energies (Wh in ÷ Wh out), not an average of per-sample ratios.
  • Harvest fraction compares mean input power in the CC phase to the panel's measured Pmax — how much of the available power the MPPT actually took.
  • Charge current dips of ~100 mA lasting one sample are the charger's own bursts; input power is flat across them.
  • Charge Ah is what entered the pack from a latched 0 V start; it is not a capacity measurement.
